Pricing calculations
- One Professional user for one month: 1 × $12 = $12.
- Five Professional users for one month: 5 × $12 = $60.
- Ten Business users for one month: 10 × $25 = $250.
- Five Professional users plus Automate in one workspace: (5 × $12) + $15 = $75.
- Ten Business users plus Advanced Insights in one workspace: (10 × $25) + $20 = $270.
- Five Professional users plus both add-ons in one workspace: (5 × $12) + $15 + $20 = $95.
These are arithmetic examples, not quotes or guarantees of the final invoice. The actual amount may depend on taxes, discounts, credits, proration, or other documented charges.

Plan selection guidance
Starter is suitable when two active projects are sufficient. Professional is a paid per-user option for routine team work. Business costs more per user, but its exact feature differences must be checked before purchase. Consider Automate only after confirming its current feature list. Consider Advanced Insights only after confirming the reporting capabilities needed. Do not pressure users to upgrade or claim an undocumented feature is available.
